# The Grand Alliance

**The Grand Alliance** is a large-scale expansion for **Heroes of Might and Magic III**, created for the **VCMI** engine.

The project combines original work with content originating from several major Heroes III modding projects, bringing many towns, creatures, heroes, artifacts, skills and gameplay changes together into one large-scale experience.

## Features

- **14 playable factions**
- Classic Heroes III factions
- Cove
- Factory
- Bulwark
- Highlands
- Forge
- Expanded creature rosters
- Double Tier 7 system for all factions (except Cove)
- New creatures and neutral units
- New heroes
- New artifacts
- New spells
- New secondary skills
- Runes and Runestone mechanics
- Numerous balance and gameplay changes
- Integration and rebalancing of content from multiple Heroes III projects

## Factions

The project includes the original nine Heroes III factions together with:

- **Cove**
- **Factory**
- **Bulwark**
- **Highlands**
- **Forge**

## Creature System

The Grand Alliance significantly expands the original army system.

Most factions receive eight different creatures, with several factions receiving two independent Tier 7 creatures.

The project also introduces and rebalances creatures originating from several community projects, including HotA, Highlands, Forge, Forge2k and Tides of War.

## Heroes

The project adds numerous heroes with new creature specialties and other gameplay roles.

This includes original heroes created for The Grand Alliance as well as heroes and content originating from other credited projects.

## Spells and Skills

The Grand Alliance introduces or modifies several gameplay mechanics, including:

- Divine Arrows
- Vitality
- Hexes
- Intimidation
- Runes
- Interference
- Resistance
- Changes to Eagle Eye
- Changes to Learning
- Support for up to 10 secondary skills

## Artifacts

The project includes additional artifacts and artifact combinations, as well as new Rune-based mechanics.

Examples include:

- Sword Breaker
- Third Eye
- Binoculars
- Rune of Charge
- Rune of Resistance
- Rune of Cure
- Rune of Swift
- Rune of Rage
- Rune of Etherealness
- Rune of Dragonform
- Rune of Resurrection

## VCMI

The Grand Alliance is designed for the **VCMI** engine.

VCMI is an open-source engine that recreates and expands the Heroes of Might and Magic III experience and provides support for extensive modding.
