# english

**The Grand Alliance** is an expansion for **Heroes of Might and Magic III**, created for the **VCMI** engine.

The project combines original gameplay design and balancing with content adapted from several Heroes III community projects. It brings together 14 playable factions, expanded creature rosters, the Double Tier 7 system, new heroes, spells, skills, artifacts and gameplay changes.

## Expanding the World

The Grand Alliance brings the total number of playable factions to **14**: the nine classic Heroes III factions, plus Cove, Factory, Bulwark, Forge and  Highlands.

Cove, Factory and Bulwark originate from Horn of the Abyss (HotA). Highlands originates from the Highlands Town project. Forge is adapted from the Forge project by MDT, with selected additional content originating from Forge2k.

The Grand Alliance uses dependencies and compatibility patches for supported external mods rather than distributing complete copies of those mods.

## Expanded Creature Rosters

Most factions command **eight distinct creatures**. The main design feature is the **Double Tier 7** system: two independent Tier 7 creatures and their dwellings can be available in the same town. Cove is the exception and retains its original Tier 7 roster with adjusted growth.

Additional creatures originate from Tides of War, Highlands, Forge and Forge2k. Their integration, placement, abilities and balance have been adapted for The Grand Alliance.

### Double Tier 7 highlights

- **Castle — Templar / Paladin:** a durable fighter with a powerful Destroy Undead spell and an army-wide Luck bonus.
- **Tower — Drake / Dragon Golem:** mechanical dragons with first-strike capabilities and magic resistance.
- **Inferno — Succubus / Abyssal Succubus:** ranged attackers that reduce enemy Attack and Defense, prevent ranged retaliation and apply a non-stacking penalty.
- **Stronghold — Brass Couatl / Quetzalcouatl:** flying units with broad protection against triggered spells.
- **Conflux — Triton / Abyssal Triton:** melee control units, that make almost all adjacent creatures to deal less damage; the Abyssal Triton can use Trawl twice.
- **Cove — Sea Serpent / Haspid:** retains its Tier 7 line and receives increased growth.
- **Bulwark — Snow Witch / Ice Sorceress:** fast melee units that use Rune artifacts and can freeze enemies after attacking.
- **Factory:** its eight-creature roster receives balance and ability changes, including changes to Couatl and Crimson Couatl Meditation.
- **Highlands:** its eight-creature roster is rebalanced for the expansion.
- **Forge:** adds the Juggernaut / Dreadnought line, renamed Annihilator / Terminator, alongside other creature and structure adjustments.

## Additional Creature Tier Changes

The following factions receive a new creature, though not a Tier 7 creature. Their creature tiers have been rearranged as follows:

- **Rampart:**
  - **T4 — Dryad / Oak Dryad:** a new flying support unit that can **revive** Dendroids.
  - **T5 — Pegasus / Silver Pegasus:** a flying unit that makes the enemy hero spend more **Spell Points** for each spell cast in combat.
  - **T6 — Dendroid Guard / Dendroid Soldier:** a strong but slow melee fighter that can **bind** enemies in place.
  - **T7 — Unicorn / War Unicorn:** a fast melee unit that can **blind** enemies after attacking and has **spell immunity** to level 1 and 2 spells. It also provides an **aura of magic resistance** to adjacent troops.
  - **T7 — Green Dragon / Gold Dragon.**
- **Necropolis:**
  - **T6 — Werewolf / Dire Werewolf:** a strong offensive unit permanently affected by Rage / Dire Rage. During combat, it gains **+1 (+2) Attack and Defense** each turn, stacking until the end of the battle.
  - **T7 — Black Knight / Dread Knight:** a fast melee unit that can **curse** the enemy and deal **double damage**.
  - **T7 — Bone Dragon / Ghost Dragon:** rebalanced.
- **Dungeon:**
  - **T5 — Illithid / Alhoon:** a slow melee fighter that can cast **Hypnosis** on enemy troops.
  - **T6 — Minotaur / Minotaur King:** a strong melee unit that **always** has positive morale.
  - **T7 — Manticore / Scorpicore:** a fast flying unit that can **paralyze** the enemy.
  - **T7 — Red Dragon / Black Dragon.**
- **Fortress:**
  - **T6 — Troll Hag / Troll Witch:** a ranged, **regenerating** unit that slows enemy troops.
  - **T7 — Wyvern / Wyvern Monarch:** a fast flying unit that can **poison** the enemy.
  - **T7 — Hydra / Chaos Hydra:** rebalanced.

### Additional Neutral Creatures

New neutral creatures have also been added from multiple projects, including: HotA, Highlands, Forge and Tides of War.

## Spells

- **Divine Arrows (Air Magic):** a level 3 spell that grants extra shots and removes ranged attack penalties for the rest of the battle.
- **Vitality (Water Magic):** a level 5 spell that increases the maximum health of all living allied creatures.

## Secondary Skills

- **Hexes:** reduces enemy Luck.
- **Intimidation:** reduces enemy Morale.
- **Runes:** available to heroes from Bulwark and Highlands, and to other heroes who meet the learning requirements.
- **Interference:** reduces the opposing hero's Spell Power.
- **Resistance:** re-enabled.
- **Eagle Eye** and **Learning:** adjusted to improve their competitiveness.
- Heroes can learn up to **10 secondary skills**.

The expansion adds new heroes and revises existing specialties. New heroes include:

- **Castle:** Charles — Templars / Paladins; Michael — Angels / Archangels.
- **Rampart:** Eurydis — Dryads / Oak Dryads; Ghuri — Green / Gold Dragons.
- **Tower:** Primus — Giants / Titans; Verstan — Drake / Dragon Golems.
- **Inferno:** Eyzea — Succubi; Kreevion — Devils / Arch Devils; The Evil — Horde (new specialty).
- **Necropolis:** Yaverr — Werewolves / Dire Werewolves; Quandyxius — Bone / Ghost Dragons.
- **Dungeon:** Galuum — Illithids / Alhoons; Daxaroth — Red / Black Dragons; Collivea — Medusas / Medusa Queens; Andres — Speed.
- **Stronghold:** Ergon — Brass Couatls / Quetzalcouatls; Tartarr — Behemoths / Ancient Behemoths.
- **Fortress:** Shroo — Troll Hags / Troll Witches; Thyroc — Hydras / Chaos Hydras.
- **Conflux:** revised creature-specialty scaling, including specialties for Tritons, Firebirds and Air Elementals.
- **Cove:** Skar — Nix / Nix Warriors; Alma — Sea Serpents / Haspids.
- **Factory:** Mosadino — Couatls / Crimson Couatls; Tudor — Dreadnoughts / Juggernauts.

- **Forge:** existing hero specialties are revised and added both t7 creatures with a designated hero of their specialty.


## Artifacts and Runes

Additional artifacts and combinations include:

- **Sword Breaker:** reduces enemy creature speed, Attack and Defense.
- **Third Eye:** combines the Stoic Watchman, Bird of Perception and Emblem of Cognizance; improves spell-learning chance and maximum learnable spell level.
- **Binoculars:** combines the Pendant of Second Sight, Speculum and Spyglass; grants Vision and increased sight radius.

### Rune artifacts

Runes and Runestone Shops are available to Bulwark and Highlands:

- **Rune of Charge:** increases a creature's speed.
- **Rune of Resistance:** reduces magic damage received.
- **Rune of Cure:** removes negative effects.
- **Rune of Swift:** grants an additional attack; requires Advanced Runes.
- **Rune of Rage:** increases damage; requires Advanced Runes.
- **Rune of Etherealness:** increases Defense; requires Advanced Runes.
- **Rune of Dragonform:** increases Attack and Defense and grants immunity to spell damage; requires Advanced Runes.
- **Rune of Resurrection:** resurrects eligible fallen creatures. Expert Runes are required for resurrected creatures to remain after battle.
